About Me

Hi! I am Palak, a Research Associate (and an aspiring Cognitive Neuroscientist) at the Cognitive Neurology Lab, NIMHANS, Bangalore, working under Dr. Suvarna Alladi.

My current work focuses on the sensorimotor and cognitive processes underlying skill expertise, with a broader aim of applying these findings towards the development of occupational-based, ecologically valid neuropsychological assessments for low-literate populations, particularly among Indian artisans. I am also interested in examining whether engagement in such complex-skilled activities may contribute to resilience against cognitive aging and neurodegenerative disease.

I combine psychophysics, computational modelling, and neuroimaging with ethnographic fieldwork and community-based outreach in my research.

Previously, I worked as a Junior Scientist under Dr. Pim Haselager at the Donders Institute, Radboud University, studying how spatial reference frames influence short-term memory recall using psychophysical experiments and eye-tracking.
